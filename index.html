About

But the real spark came from an unexpected place: while working as an after-school caregiver, I kept wishing I could build a simple app for the parents. That idea stuck with me, and it's a big part of why I'm now expanding into full-stack development (JavaScript, Node.js, React, and Python) to build software that makes people's lives a little easier.
